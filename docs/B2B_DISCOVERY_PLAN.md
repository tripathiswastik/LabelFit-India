# LabelFit India: B2B label-digitization discovery plan

**Status:** Interview guide and hypotheses only. No verified customer interviews, pricing signals, pilots, letters of intent, or revenue are documented in this repository.

## Questions to investigate

- How do food brands and retailers currently capture package information in product catalogs?
- Which source do they trust: approved artwork, supplier data, physical package photography, or another system?
- Where do delays or transcription errors occur, and how often?
- What does review or correction cost in staff time?
- Would a source-linked candidate transcription fit their workflow, and what human review remains necessary?
- What export, security, privacy, retention, and audit requirements would a pilot need?
- Who owns the budget, and what evidence would be required for a paid trial?

Do not lead with LabelFit's proposed savings or ask a hypothetical price question until the current workflow and costs are understood. Record exact wording with consent, date, role, organization category, interviewer, and follow-up status. Separate observed facts from interpretation; do not identify a person or organization without permission.

## Candidate interviewees

Potential segments include packaged-food brand catalog or regulatory operations, grocery marketplace catalog teams, and packaging data service providers. Named organizations are prospects only; no contact or endorsement is implied.

## Decision criteria to pre-register

After a small set of neutral discovery interviews, summarize whether the problem recurs, the current source of truth, error/review costs, adoption blockers, and whether a buyer owns the problem. Set numerical continue/pivot criteria before interviews begin. Any pricing values in future analysis must be directly attributed to documented conversations and must not be generalized from hypothetical reactions.
