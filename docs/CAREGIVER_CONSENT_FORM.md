---

## 1. Study Purpose & Nature of the Tool

You are invited to participate in a usability research study evaluating **LabelFit India**, an experimental software prototype designed to assist users in reading printed packaging information on packaged complementary foods (for infants and young children aged 6+ months).

**What LabelFit India Does:**
- Transcribes literal printed text from product packaging panels (e.g., product names, age markings, ingredients, allergens, preparation directions, and nutrition panels).
- Displays short verbatim photo snippets to assist the user in visually verifying what is written on the physical packaging.
- Provides status indicators (`Readable`, `Check text`, `Not visible`) reflecting the clarity of the machine's text transcription.

**What LabelFit India DOES NOT Do (Critical Boundary):**
- LabelFit India **does NOT** provide medical, paediatric, clinical, nutritional, or infant feeding advice.
- LabelFit India **does NOT** verify whether a food product is safe, healthy, allergen-free, or suitable for any individual child.
- A status of `Readable` means only that printed packaging characters were clearly recognized by the optical model. It **never** means the product is approved, safe, or recommended for feeding.

---

## 2. Participant Eligibility Criteria

To participate, you must meet all of the following criteria:
1. You are at least 18 years of age (legal adult).
2. You are a parent, guardian, or primary caregiver who regularly purchases or prepares food for infants or young children.
3. You agree to inspect physical retail packages in person during this session.
4. **Zero Child Involvement**: No infants or children will participate in or be present during this evaluation session.

---

## 3. Session Procedure

- **Duration**: Approximately 25–35 minutes.
- **Tasks**: You will be asked to locate 4 specific pieces of printed packaging information (e.g., stated age suitability, allergen declarations, preparation water instructions, and small/curved text) on authentic retail infant food packages.
- **Comparison**: For some packages, you will inspect the physical box alone using a stopwatch; for others, you will use the physical box alongside the prototype display.
- **Feedback**: You will be asked short interview questions regarding your understanding of the display badges and how you used the physical packaging.

---

## 4. Privacy, Data Protection & Confidentiality (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:
1. **Consent-Based Processing**: Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty or explanation.
2. **No PII Publication**: Your name, contact details, signature, and any identifying information will be collected solely for consent verification and stored in an offline, password-protected, restricted archive.
3. **Anonymized Research Identifiers**: All timing data, task observations, and interview responses will be referenced exclusively through random research identifiers (e.g., `CG-001`, `CG-002`). No personal identifiers will ever appear in research reports, code repositories, or public pitch presentations.
4. **Ephemeral Processing**: No photographs of you or your personal devices are captured. Only physical packaging cartons are examined.
5. **Right to Correction and Erasure**: You have the right to request a summary of the data recorded about your session or request the immediate deletion of your session records by contacting the study coordinator.
